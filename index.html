JPC VISUALS / APP INFORMATION

JPC Studio
YouTube

A private, single-user tool that JPC Visuals uses to prepare, upload and check videos on its own YouTube channel.

JPC Studio YouTube uses the YouTube API Services. It is operated by and for JPC Visuals only. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up or Google sign-in on these pages.

Read the privacy policy →

01 / PURPOSE

What the app does

JPC Studio YouTube connects the JPC Visuals YouTube channel to the owner's private publishing workflow. With the owner's authorization, it can:

  • read the channel's basic information and its list of videos, to confirm the right channel is connected;
  • upload a video the owner has approved, with the title, description, visibility and other settings the owner has approved;
  • check an upload's processing and publication status afterwards; and
  • read comments on the channel's videos and, only when the owner approves a specific reply, post that reply.

An AI assistant helps draft titles, descriptions and replies and runs the steps above. It suggests; the owner decides. Nothing is uploaded, published, replied to or deleted on YouTube without the owner's approval of that specific action.

02 / ACCESS

Authorization & control

The only Google account authorized to use this app is the JPC Visuals owner's account. Authorization happens through Google's own OAuth consent screen, which lists the permissions requested. The app never asks for or stores a Google password.

Some Google permissions are broader than the actions listed above; the app is used only for those actions. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account › Third-party connections. Revoking access does not remove videos already published to YouTube.
