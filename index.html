JPC VISUALS / APP INFORMATION

JPC Studio
YouTube

A private, owner-operated workflow for preparing and publishing JPC Visuals videos on YouTube.

This is an information site, not the publishing workspace. There is no public sign-up or Google sign-in on these pages.

Draft for operator review. The privacy notice is not finalized for Google authorization. Data-retention, deletion and AI-provider controls still need verification.

Review the draft privacy notice ↗

01 / PURPOSE

What the app does

JPC Studio YouTube connects the JPC Visuals operator’s authorized YouTube channel to a controlled publishing workflow. It helps prepare video titles and descriptions, upload operator-approved media, and check the channel and publication results.

The private workflow uses Hermes and Composio. AI assistance may be used for the requested preparation or verification task; it is not a public chat service.

02 / ACCESS

Authorization & control

Google authorization takes place separately through the integration’s OAuth consent flow. Access is intended for the operator’s approved business channel, not for visitors to this website.

The Google permission screen describes the permissions being granted. Some permissions can be broader than the actions enabled in the current workflow. Website access does not grant channel access, and revoking an integration does not automatically remove videos already published to YouTube.

See data access, revocation & deletion information
