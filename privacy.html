JPC STUDIO YOUTUBE / PRIVACY

Privacy policy (draft)

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy describes the private YouTube publishing workflow operated for JPC Visuals, and these public information pages.

Draft — not a finalized authorization notice. Data-retention and deletion controls, the configured AI provider’s handling, and operational safeguards still need verification. This draft must not be used as evidence that the app meets Google or YouTube requirements.

1. Scope & operator

JPC Studio YouTube is a private, owner-operated publishing workflow for JPC Visuals. It is not offered as a public sign-up service. The operator is responsible for deciding which accounts, content and publishing actions are authorized.

Privacy and support contact: jpcvisualspro@gmail.com.

2. Data the workflow processes

  • Channel and video information: information returned by authorized YouTube API Services calls, such as channel identifiers and names, video identifiers, titles, descriptions, publication and processing status, and requested channel or video statistics.
  • Operator-provided content: selected video and audio files, thumbnails when supplied, captions or descriptive text, publishing instructions, review decisions and approvals.
  • Authorization and operational records: connection details, OAuth credentials managed by the integration service, tool results, publication receipts and troubleshooting records. Secrets are not part of these public web pages.

The data involved depends on the action requested. Viewing this website does not connect a Google account or authorize access to a YouTube channel.

3. Why data is used

The workflow uses data to identify the authorized channel, prepare the requested publishing materials, upload approved content, verify publication results, and investigate operational problems. AI assistance may process relevant prompts, selected content and tool results for the requested workflow.

This description is not a claim that Google has verified or endorsed the app. Google’s consent screen remains the source for the permissions requested from the operator.

4. Services involved in processing

  • Hermes: runs the operator’s configured workflow and can keep local conversations, working files, approvals and tool records.
  • Composio: manages the authorized service connection and executes integration actions. Selected publishing files may be transferred or staged through the configured integration path.
  • Google and YouTube: provide OAuth authorization, YouTube API Services, channel information and video hosting.
  • The configured AI provider: can receive workflow prompts, selected content and tool results when AI assistance is used. The provider and its data-handling settings depend on the operator’s configuration.

Third-party services have their own privacy practices and retention arrangements. This policy does not promise that every provider has zero retention, that AI processing stays on the operator’s computer, or that unverified provider settings prevent all model training. Contact the operator for the current configuration before authorizing an account.

Relevant service information: Google Privacy Policy, YouTube Terms of Service, and Composio Privacy Policy.

5. Storage & retention

Working media, prepared copy, approvals, conversations and operational logs can be stored in the operator’s environment. Local records may include data returned by an integration. They can remain until the operator removes them; no automatic deletion schedule is asserted here.

Connected services may retain authorization records, uploads or logs under their own policies and account settings. Removing a local copy does not by itself delete copies held by another service. A retention or deletion request should identify the relevant account or channel so the operator can determine which records and services are involved.

6. Access, revocation & deletion requests

The operator can review or revoke Google authorization through Google account permissions. Revoking access stops the integration from relying on that authorization; it does not automatically erase existing workflow records or previously published YouTube videos.

To request access to or deletion of stored workflow data, email jpcvisualspro@gmail.com with the app name and relevant channel or account information. Identity and authority may need to be verified before records are changed. Do not include a password, OAuth token, or verification code.

Requests to remove videos from YouTube are separate from requests to delete local workflow records and require the channel owner’s authorization.

7. These information pages

The website files contain no sign-in form, payment form, analytics script, advertising script, or embedded video. They do not store information in browser local storage or set application cookies.

When these files are served by GitHub Pages, GitHub may process ordinary hosting-request information. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Following an external link or contacting the operator by email involves the practices of that destination or email provider.

8. Questions & updates

Contact jpcvisualspro@gmail.com for privacy questions, support, or clarification about the current workflow. Changes to the workflow or hosting arrangement may require an update to this page; the date above identifies this version.
