JPC STUDIO YOUTUBE / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what JPC Studio YouTube accesses through the YouTube API Services, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and how to revoke access or ask for deletion.

1. Who this policy covers

JPC Studio YouTube ("the app") is a private, single-user tool operated by JPC Visuals. Its only user is the JPC Visuals owner, who authorizes it to act on the JPC Visuals YouTube channel. The app is not offered to the public and has no public sign-up.

The owner must agree to this policy and to the YouTube Terms of Service before using the app (see Terms of use). Because the app reads comments on the channel's videos, this policy also describes how public information from people who comment is handled.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API) to access the JPC Visuals channel. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's collection and use of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy (also at www.google.com/policies/privacy).

This policy is not a statement that Google has reviewed, verified or endorsed the app.

3. Information the app accesses

From YouTube, with the owner's authorization:

  • channel identity: the channel's ID, name and basic profile details;
  • the channel's videos: video IDs, titles, descriptions, tags, visibility, processing and publication status, and video statistics such as view counts when the owner asks for them;
  • comments on the channel's videos: comment text, the commenter's public display name and channel ID, and the comment date.

Provided by the owner: video and thumbnail files selected for upload, draft and approved titles, descriptions and replies, publishing instructions, and the owner's approval decisions.

Authorization data: the OAuth access and refresh tokens Google issues when the owner grants access. The app never asks for, receives or stores a Google or YouTube password.

Permissions requested from Google and why:

  • youtube.upload (manage your YouTube videos): to upload videos the owner has approved.
  • youtube.readonly (view your YouTube account): to confirm the connected channel and check videos and upload status.
  • youtube.force-ssl (see, edit and permanently delete your YouTube videos, ratings, comments and captions): to read comments and post owner-approved replies, and to update a video's details when the owner asks. This permission is broader than these uses; the app does not delete videos or comments or change ratings unless the owner approves that specific action.

The app does not request YouTube Analytics, YouTube Reporting, Gmail, Drive or other Google data. The app does not download or store copies of YouTube videos.

4. How the information is used

Information is used only to run the owner's own publishing workflow: to confirm the right channel is connected, prepare and upload approved videos, check that uploads were processed and published as intended, read comments and post replies the owner approves, and fix problems with these steps.

The AI assistant may suggest titles, descriptions and replies. The owner has final control over everything sent to YouTube, including the visibility setting (public, unlisted or private) of each upload. Nothing is uploaded, published, edited, replied to or deleted on YouTube without the owner's approval of that specific action.

Information from YouTube is not sold, is not used for advertising, is not combined with data from other channels, and is not used for any purpose other than the ones listed here. The app does not show advertising or let third parties serve content.

5. Who receives the information

The information described above is shared only with the service providers that run the workflow for the owner. Grok Bot is the only AI assistant service the app currently uses.

  • Google / YouTube: receives the uploads, video details and replies the owner approves, and provides the channel information and OAuth authorization.
  • Composio (integration service): holds the OAuth tokens for the connection, makes the YouTube API calls the workflow requests, and may temporarily stage upload files on the way to YouTube. See the Composio Privacy Policy.
  • Grok Bot (AI assistant service): the owner's AI assistants run in this service. They receive the owner's instructions, files selected for a task, drafts, and the results of YouTube API calls, including the channel, video and comment information in section 3. Working files and conversation history are stored in the owner's Grok Bot account and workspace. How long the service keeps that data, and whether it may be used to improve the service's AI models, is governed by the service's own terms and privacy policy, not by this policy; this policy makes no separate promise about either.
  • Earlier local workflow: the owner previously used a different AI agent on the owner's own computer for this work. It is no longer used for YouTube. Any YouTube information remaining in its past records is being deleted under the 30-day rule in section 6.
  • GitHub hosts these information pages (see section 9).

Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. Information may also be disclosed if required by law. It is not shared with anyone else.

6. Retention & deletion

The app keeps information only as long as needed for the purposes in section 4, within these limits from the YouTube API Services Developer Policies:

  • YouTube information (channel, video and comment details copied into working notes, logs or conversations) is deleted or refreshed from YouTube within 30 days of being retrieved.
  • Video statistics such as view counts may be kept longer, but only while the owner's authorization is still active; it is rechecked at least every 30 days.
  • OAuth tokens are kept by Composio only while the connection is active and are removed when the connection is deleted.
  • The owner's own materials (original media files, drafts and approvals) are kept until the owner deletes them.

How this is carried out: at least once every 30 days the owner reviews the app's working files, notes and logs and deletes or refreshes any YouTube information older than 30 days.

Deletion requests: email jpcvisualspro@gmail.com to ask for stored information to be deleted. It will be deleted as soon as possible and within 7 days. Copies held by a provider in section 5 are deleted using that provider's own deletion tools, under its own timelines.

Deleting information stored by the app does not affect anything stored on YouTube. To delete videos or comments on YouTube itself, use YouTube Studio or another YouTube application that supports deletion.

7. Revoking access

The app's access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (also reachable from Google Account › Security › Third-party connections). The owner can also disconnect YouTube inside Composio, which removes the stored tokens.

After access is revoked, YouTube information obtained through that authorization is deleted within 7 days, in addition to the normal deletion process in section 6. Revoking access does not remove videos already published to YouTube.

8. Security

  • Access uses Google OAuth. No Google or YouTube password is requested or stored.
  • OAuth tokens are held by Composio. They are not published on this website and are not shared in chat or email.
  • Only the owner, and the AI assistants the owner has set up, can use the connection.
  • The Google account that owns the app's Google Cloud project is protected with 2-Step Verification.
  • Calls to the YouTube API use HTTPS.
  • Every publishing action requires the owner's specific approval.

No system is completely secure, and this policy does not promise more protection than is listed here.

9. This website

These information pages have no sign-in, forms, analytics, advertising, embedded videos or scripts. They do not set cookies or store anything on your device.

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

10. Changes & contact

If the app starts to use information in a new way or for a new purpose, this policy will be updated first and the owner will re-accept it before the new use begins. The effective date at the top shows the current version.

Questions or complaints about this policy or the app's privacy practices: jpcvisualspro@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, tokens or verification codes.
